Always Ahead. Never Visible.

Executive protection shaped by intelligence. Calibrated to your risk.

AYC Security provides executive and close protection services built on intelligence, advance planning, and discretion. Armed and unarmed. Uniformed and plainclothes. Each protection program is shaped by the individual's risk profile and monitored throughout by our 24/7 Global Security Operations Center.

Who We Protect

  • Corporate executives and C-suite principals
  • High-profile individuals and public figures
  • Political figures and diplomatic personnel
  • Organizations at elevated-risk events
  • Families and estate principals

Close protection is not about presence alone. The most important work happens before the client moves: threat assessment, advance work, route analysis, venue verification. What appears effortless is the result of preparation they never see.

From low-profile personal security to full protection details, every program is built around a specific risk picture, operational environment, and daily requirements. No two assignments are the same.

What We Provide

Executive Protection Services

From single-event VIP coverage to ongoing personal security programs, AYC provides close protection scaled to the assignment and the threat environment.

Personal Security Details

Dedicated protection personnel scaled to the assignment and the risk environment. Plainclothes or uniformed officers based on the individual's preference.

Protective Drivers & Vehicle Security

Vetted drivers with security training and advance route planning.

Estate & Residential Security

Security programs for private residences and family compounds, calibrated to the household's schedule and daily rhythm.

Travel Security

Pre-travel intelligence, in-country coordination, and airport logistics. Operations center monitoring throughout. Domestic and international.

Event & Venue Close Protection

Advance work, venue assessment, and dedicated officer presence for high-profile event appearances and public-facing engagements.

24/7 Operations Center Support

All assignments are supported by our 24/7 Global Security Operations Center with persistent monitoring and crisis response.
