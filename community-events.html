Not every security situation looks the same. A school or house of worship needs a presence that is protective yet welcoming. A corporate gala calls for officers who are polished, discreet, and calibrated to the environment.

AYC provides security that fits the situation. Armed and unarmed. Uniformed and plainclothes. Same-day deployment available. Every deployed officer is monitored through our Global Security Operations Center with GPS-confirmed hourly check-ins.

Community Security

Where community gathers,
we stand watch.

Religious institutions, schools, and community organizations share something in common: the people inside them should feel safe to gather, without feeling surveilled. AYC provides security that protects what they have built, without disrupting the warmth and openness that defines those spaces.

Our officers are prepared for environments where the tone of the security presence matters as much as the preparation behind it. We have worked with religious communities across South Florida, including synagogues, churches and mosques, and understand the specific security considerations each community faces.

  • Shabbat, High Holiday, and year-round synagogue security
  • Church, mosque, and multi-faith community security
  • School and campus security
  • Cultural institutions and community organizations
  • Cemetery and memorial services
  • Access control and perimeter monitoring
  • GSOC-monitored hourly officer check-ins

How It Works

01

Tell Us About Your Event or Community

Call, text, or fill out the form. Tell us about your community, event or security need. We will ask the right questions, from venue and expected attendance to the type of security presence you want on site. For same-day or urgent requests, call or text. We respond the same day.

02

We Build the Right Coverage

We match the right personnel to your environment, armed or unarmed, uniformed or plainclothes, based on venue, attendance, and the tone you need. For ongoing community deployments, staffing is aligned to your schedule and facility.

03

Covered and Monitored

Every officer checks in with GPS confirmation every hour through our Global Security Operations Center, verified by a live human operator. You have a direct line to our leadership team before, during, and after the engagement.
